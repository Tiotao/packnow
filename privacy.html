Privacy Policy

PackNow · Last updated October 19, 2026

Summary

PackNow does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your trips, packing lists, templates, categories, preferences and the habits PackNow learns from your past trips are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Services provided by Apple

To work, PackNow uses two Apple services. The developer of PackNow does not receive any of this information.

  • Apple Maps – when you search for a destination, the text you type is sent to Apple to find matching places.
  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit) – the coordinates of your destinations and the trip dates are sent to Apple to get a forecast. PackNow does not use your current location.

Apple's handling of this data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy. Weather data is provided by Apple Weather.

Children

PackNow does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
