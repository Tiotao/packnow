Frequently asked questions

How does PackNow build my list?

It starts from the template you pick, adds a few items for the weather at each destination, then adds your always-pack rules and items you've packed on past trips.

Why don't I see a weather forecast?

Forecasts come from Apple Weather and cover about the next 10 days. For trips further out, PackNow uses last year's weather for the same dates. Without a connection you can set the weather by hand when creating the trip.

Can I plan a trip with several cities?

Yes. When creating a trip, add more stops. Each stop has its own dates and weather, and everything goes into one combined list.

How do I edit or remove an item?

Swipe a row right to edit it, or left to remove it. A long swipe left removes it straight away.

Can I make my own templates and categories?

Yes, in Settings → Templates and Settings → Categories.

Is my data backed up?

Your trips and lists are stored only on your iPhone and are included in your regular iPhone backups. PackNow has no account and doesn't sync to a server.

Which languages are supported?

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Spanish, French and German. PackNow follows your iPhone's language, or the language set for PackNow in the iPhone Settings app.
